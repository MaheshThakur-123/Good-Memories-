Now TodayA letter for you

Priti, I'm sorry

I understand what it took for you to go from nothing to letting me into your life and developing feelings. Jo aapke liye bahut scary aur kind of a nightmare hai. But still, you chose me, for my happiness.

And I understood where I went wrong. I should handle my own emotions instead of making them a burden on you. I should enjoy our journey instead of turning it into pressure for you, and let things happen in their own natural way. I should respect your space.

Honestly, I do respect you. It's just that because of my over-love, things didn't happen the way they should have. I never wanted to bother you. Bas aapke case mein main bahut emotional ho jaata hoon.

I understood my biggest problem too. When I'm angry, I say stupid things like "end it". That was really pathetic of me. Uske liye mujhe maar lo, but mujhe apne se door mat karo. Every single day, I miss you.

I'm really sorry for anything that hurt you.

And I'm sorry for what I said very recently. I'm really, really, really sorry. It was a huge mistake, and I understand that now.

SORRY FOR EVERYTHING.
PLEASE FORGIVE ME.
